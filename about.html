About Me

Background, interests and education.

I'm Mohammad, a final year MEng Aero-Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Strathclyde.

I got into engineering because I've always enjoyed building things and working out why they do or don't work. Cars and planes were what first drew me towards Aero-Mechanical Engineering, since it covers both. That said, the design and problem solving side of the job applies just about anywhere, so I'm open to working across any industry.

Through my degree I've specialised in mechanical design, simulation and testing. I'm passionate about working in a team, and a lot of my experience has come from multidisciplinary environments. For my thesis I collaborated with Airbus on the icing certification of an eVTOL aircraft, working alongside the design and manufacturing groups throughout. I also project managed a group build of an RC aircraft, and my placements have been in chemical manufacturing and maritime engineering. Moving between such different industries has helped me fit into multidisciplinary teams and broaden my expertise along the way.

Outside of university I spend a lot of my time hiking, which is how I get outdoors and clear my head. I'm also a big comic book reader, and I'll happily lose an afternoon to a good run of issues.
