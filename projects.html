Year 4 Thesis Project

Icing Certification for Urban Air Mobility Systems

Airbus · OSCAR Programme

  • ANSYS Fluent
  • eVTOL
  • Certification
  • Aerodynamics
  • Worked within the certification work package of the Airbus OSCAR Programme, collaborating with the design and manufacturing teams to deliver an Appendix C icing certification strategy for an eVTOL aircraft.
  • Constructed a certification compliance matrix consolidating the Appendix C icing requirements of four regulatory frameworks (CS-23, SC-VTOL, CS-P and SC E-19) into 13 top-level requirements and 110 Means of Compliance sub-requirements, each mapped to testing methodologies to minimise certification cost and time.
  • Assessed the aerodynamic penalty of worst-case Appendix C conditions on the OSCAR eVTOL aerofoil in ANSYS Fluent using scaling methodologies from existing literature. The resulting degradation figures were relayed to the design team to inform the next design iteration.
  • Evaluated active and passive ice protection and ice detection technologies against six criteria, with an ICE-WIPS hybrid system coupled with polyurethane and low surface energy coatings recommended across different regions of the aircraft.

Year 4 Coursework Project

Smokestack Chimney CFD Analysis

Computational Fluid Dynamics

  • ANSYS Fluent
  • CFD
  • Natural Convection
  • Analysed a 2D heat chamber-chimney system in ANSYS Fluent, modelling buoyancy driven natural convection through the Boussinesq approximation.
  • Established grid independence through a comprehensive mesh refinement study, selecting a second order discretisation scheme to balance numerical accuracy against computational demands.
  • Performed a critical solver analysis (pressure vs. density-based), utilising non dimensional parameters to validate the flow physics against existing literature.
Year 4 Coursework Project

FEA Study of a Bracket

Structural Analysis & Optimisation

  • SOLIDWORKS
  • ANSYS Workbench
  • MATLAB
  • FEA
  • Optimised the geometry of an S355J2+N bracket using SOLIDWORKS and ANSYS Workbench, reducing component mass by 32.5% while keeping the safety factor above the 2.5 design target.
  • Executed linear elastic FEA with global and local mesh convergence studies to minimise computational demand and obtain accurate elastic behaviour. This model was validated against Euler-Bernoulli beam theory through a MATLAB script.
  • Conducted a high-cycle fatigue assessment against imported S-N curve data, confirming the modified design remained below the endurance limit for infinite life at the 100 Hz operating frequency.
  • Carried out modal and nonlinear limit load analyses under bilinear kinematic hardening assumptions to ensure sufficient safety margins against resonance and to determine the plastic limit load prior to structural fracture.
